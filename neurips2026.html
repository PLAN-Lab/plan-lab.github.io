Robot learning in a changing world

Friday, December 11 · Sydney, Australia

LIBERO-MAX: Do Robot Policies Adapt When the World Changes?

LIBERO-MAX evaluates whether robot policies maintain task success when the environment changes after execution begins, using matched rollouts to isolate the effects of dynamic changes.

Presented at Robot Learning with World Models: Capabilities, Frontiers, and Challenges.

Individual presentation time and poster location: to be announced.
